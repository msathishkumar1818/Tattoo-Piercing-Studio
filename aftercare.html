Self-Evaluation Guide
Normal Healing
  • ✓ Clear or pale straw-colored fluid that dries into light crusties
  • ✓ Mild localized tenderness during the first 7–14 days
  • ✓ Gradual reduction in swelling each subsequent week
Seek Studio Help
  • ✕ Thick green, dark yellow, or foul-smelling continuous discharge
  • ✕ Throbbing radiating heat with spreading redness past the piercing
  • ✕ Systemic fever, chills, or escalating severe sharp pain
