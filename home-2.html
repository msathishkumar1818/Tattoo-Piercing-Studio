Protocol & Process

The Three-Phase Atelier Standard

From initial bone structure mapping to medical-grade healing, our procedural protocol guarantees safety and permanence.

01

Anatomy and Placement

Your artist evaluates muscular movement, skin tone, and body angles to ensure the artwork breathes naturally with your body.

02

Autoclave & Cleanroom

Individual surgical barrier barriers applied before unsealing sterile EO-gas-treated needle cartridges in front of the client.

03

Dermal Care & Healing

Application of breathable second-skin dermal bandages alongside comprehensive aftercare documentation and follow-up checks.
